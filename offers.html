Offer Terms & Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using promotional codes, stacking discounts, and redeeming deals.

How do I redeem my promotional voucher code?

Simply copy any coupon code (such as CRISP50 or FEAST20) using our one-click copy button, open your Takeaway Bag by clicking the "Bag" icon in the top header, paste the code into the promo input box, and press Apply! Your subtotal will immediately reflect the discount.

Can I combine multiple promo codes on a single order?

Only one percentage discount promo code may be applied per takeaway order. However, special bundle discounts (like our 16-Pc Mega Party Deal) can be ordered alongside promo codes for maximum savings!

Are these offers valid for both counter pickup and curbside delivery?

Yes! All deals showcased on this page are 100% valid across all 3 Crispster takeaway locations for express counter pick-up and drive-up curbside handover.

How often do new offers and flash drops arrive?

We refresh our limited-time promotions every Tuesday and Friday at midnight. Follow our social media or check back on this page whenever our live countdown timer reaches zero!
