Chef's Kitchen Secret of the Week

Never Cover Hot Fried Chicken in an Air-Tight Container!

"The moment hot fried chicken is sealed without ventilation, the trapped steam condenses on the golden crust and reverses the Maillard reaction, turning your crunchy dredge into wet paste. Always use calibrated vented containers — which is why every Crispster box has laser-cut thermal exhaust ports!"
