Instant Answers

Help & Store FAQs

Quick information regarding curbside collection, contactless pickup, accepted payments, and advance party notice.

How does Crispster's Curbside Express Pickup work?

When placing your order online or by phone, select Curbside Pickup and note your vehicle make/color. Pull into our designated numbered bays outside our store, tap "I'm Here" on your phone confirmation, and a Crispster team member will bring your piping-hot thermal order directly to your window!

What payment methods are accepted at counter pickup?

We accept all major debit & credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, tap-to-pay mobile wallets, and cash at all 3 counter locations.

How much advance notice is required for party catering orders?

For party boxes of 50 pieces or less, you can order directly through our standard takeaway line with just 30–45 minutes notice. For large corporate events or 100+ piece catering, we recommend giving us 24 hours advance notice via our contact form or catering hotline.

Can I customize the spiciness level of chicken pieces in a bucket?

Absolutely! You can choose Half Mild / Half Nashville Fire on any bucket of 8 pieces or larger, or try our interactive "Build Your Own Bucket" tool on our Menu page to select your exact preference.
