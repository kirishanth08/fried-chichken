Honest Food Philosophy

Ingredient & Allergen Care

We believe you deserve full honesty about what goes into your food. Sourced responsibly and prepared under strict quality standards.

100% Peanut-Free Kitchen

We only fry in premium refined canola-vegetable oil blends. Absolutely no peanut or tree nut oils on site.

Certified Halal Poultry

All chicken across all Crispster locations is 100% certified halal, ethical, and hormone-free.

Gluten Transparency

Our chicken dredge contains wheat flour. For gluten-conscious guests, our slaw and grilled options are available upon phone request.

Cultured Dairy Marinade

We soak in real cultured buttermilk for tenderness. Non-dairy tenders are available at our downtown hub.
