Our Humble Beginnings

From Street Cart To Cult Phenomenon

In 2018, our founder Chef Marcus Vance opened a tiny curbside fryer shack with a single deep vat and an experimental 12-spice buttermilk brine. Queues began wrapping around the block within weeks. Foodies couldn't get enough of the shattering crunch and deep, savory herbal flavor.

Today, Crispster operates across 3 bustling hubs with express counter collection and curbside bays, but our founding philosophy has never wavered: fresh whole poultry, never frozen, bathed in buttermilk for 24 hours, and fried precisely to 375°F.

Our Core Standards

The 4 Pillars of Crispster Quality

We don't cut corners. Every drumstick, tender, and wing adheres to these non-negotiable kitchen principles.

1. Ethically Sourced Poultry

We partner exclusively with certified humane, antibiotic-free regional poultry farms. Poultry arrives chilled daily and never touches a commercial freezer.

2. 24-Hour Cultured Brine

True flavor takes patience. Soaking in whole cultured buttermilk with sea salt and 12 crushed botanicals breaks down proteins for succulent tenderness.

3. Triple-Dredge Technique

Each piece is hand-dredged three distinct times in seasoned unbleached flour, creating thousands of micro-craters that trap crunch and vaporize surface steam.

4. 375°F Flash Frying

High-smoke vegetable oils seal the crust within seconds, forming an impenetrable barrier that keeps grease out and rich juices sealed inside.

The Culinary Process

From Farm To Takeaway Counter

See how your chicken goes from morning delivery to blazing hot takeaway perfection in just 4 disciplined steps.

01

6:00 AM Farm Delivery

Chilled, certified halal poultry arrives fresh from our partner farms. Inspected for weight, color, and cut precision.

02

24-Hour Herb Bath

Chicken cuts submerge into massive stainless steel vats of cultured buttermilk and 12 aromatic herbs to tenderize overnight.

03

Triple Hand-Dredge

Cooks toss, press, and shake each drumstick and breast fillet through seasoned flour 3 times to build microscopic ridges.

04

12-Minute Pickup

Flash fried to golden bronze at 375°F, packaged in vented thermo-boxes, and ready at our counter in 12–15 minutes flat.
